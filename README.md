📊 Larry Jay Supermarket Sales & Profit Analysis

📌 Project Overview
This project is an independent Sales and Profitability Analysis completed using Microsoft Excel and the Larry Jay Supermarket Sales dataset.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into an interactive dashboard that provides insights into sales performance, profitability, customer segments, products, countries, discount strategies, and sales trends over time.

The project demonstrates how Excel can be used to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that can support data-driven decision-making.

🎯 Business Objectives
The analysis was designed to answer key business questions such as:

How are overall sales and profits performing?
Which customer segments generate the highest sales and profit?
Which segments generate significant sales but weaker profitability?
Which products are the strongest contributors to performance?
Which countries demonstrate stronger sales performance?
How do discount levels relate to profitability?
How does sales performance change over time?
Which areas of the business may require management attention?

🔎 Key Insights
1. Government Segment Generated the Strongest Overall Performance
The Government customer segment generated the highest sales and profit among the analysed customer segments.

This indicates that the Government segment is an important contributor to the company’s overall financial performance.

Business implication:
The business should continue monitoring and understanding the factors contributing to the segment’s strong performance while maintaining sustainable profitability.

2. Enterprise Segment Requires Profitability Attention
The Enterprise segment generated significant sales but recorded a loss.

This is an important business finding because strong revenue generation does not necessarily translate into profitability.

The result suggests that the underlying economics of Enterprise sales may require further investigation.

Potential areas for review include:

Pricing strategies
Discount levels
Operating costs
Product mix
Customer-specific costs
Profit margins
Business implication:
Management could investigate why relatively high sales are not translating into positive profit within the Enterprise segment.

3. Paseo Was the Best-Performing Product
Paseo emerged as the best-performing product in the analysis.

Its performance makes it an important contributor to overall business performance and provides an opportunity to understand the factors driving its success.

Business implication:
The company could examine Paseo’s pricing, demand, customer segments and sales distribution to identify practices that could potentially be applied to other products.

4. Japan, France and England Recorded Strong Sales Performance
Among the countries included in the analysis, Japan, France and England recorded the strongest sales performance.

This demonstrates that sales performance varies geographically, with some markets contributing more strongly to overall revenue.

Business implication:
Further analysis could explore differences in customer composition, product demand, discounting patterns and purchasing behaviour across markets.

5. Lower Discount Levels Were Associated With Higher Profit
The analysis showed that lower discount levels generated higher profits.

This highlights the importance of balancing sales volume with profitability. While discounts can encourage purchases and increase sales, excessive discounting can reduce the profit generated from those sales.

Business implication:
The company could review discount policies and assess whether discounts are generating sufficient additional sales to justify their impact on margins.

6. Profitability Was Not Evenly Distributed
Although the company generated strong overall sales and positive profit, profitability was not evenly distributed across all customer segments and business areas.

This demonstrates why businesses should analyse both revenue and profit rather than relying on sales alone as a measure of performance.

A segment can generate substantial revenue while still negatively affecting profitability.

7. Sales Peaked Around July in Both Years
The time-series analysis showed that sales peaked around July in both years represented in the dataset.

This recurring pattern may indicate a seasonal or cyclical component to sales performance.

Business implication:
The business could investigate the factors behind the July peak and use historical patterns to support inventory planning, promotional activities, staffing and sales forecasting.

💡 Business Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the following areas could be considered:

1. Maintain Government Segment Performance
Continue monitoring the Government segment because of its contribution to both sales and profit.

2. Investigate Enterprise Losses
Conduct a deeper profitability review of Enterprise customers to identify whether pricing, discounting, costs or product mix are contributing to the losses.

3. Review Discount Strategies
Evaluate discount levels by customer segment and product to identify opportunities to improve profitability without unnecessarily reducing sales volume.

4. Analyse Paseo’s Success
Investigate the factors contributing to Paseo’s strong performance and assess whether similar strategies could support other products.

5. Analyse Geographic Performance
Explore why Japan, France and England are performing strongly and whether similar market characteristics exist in lower-performing countries.

6. Incorporate Seasonal Trends
Use the recurring July sales peak as a starting point for further investigation into seasonal demand and planning.

📌 Key Takeaway
The analysis demonstrates that strong sales performance does not necessarily translate into equally strong profitability across every area of a business.

The company generated total sales of 118,726,349 and total profit of 16,893,699.

However, the detailed analysis revealed significant differences across customer segments, products, countries and discount levels.

The Government segment and Paseo product were identified as important contributors to performance, while the Enterprise segment generated significant sales but recorded losses.

The analysis therefore highlights the importance of looking beyond total revenue and examining profitability, customer segments, product performance, discount strategies and trends over time when making business decisions.
